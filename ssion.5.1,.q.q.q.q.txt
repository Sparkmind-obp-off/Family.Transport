SESSION 5-G — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

5-G.1 — ARCHITECTURE DECISION

Untuk Family Transport MVP, kita gunakan prinsip:

> Modular Monolith + API-first + Cloudflare-compatible.



Bukan microservices.

Kenapa?

Family Transport masih satu business domain. Microservices sekarang hanya menambah:

deployment complexity

network boundaries

observability burden

authentication complexity

debugging complexity


Jadi:

ONE APPLICATION
     │
     ├── Public Web
     ├── Customer
     ├── Admin
     └── API
           │
           ├── Auth
           ├── Booking
           ├── Availability
           ├── Pricing
           ├── Quote
           ├── Payment
           ├── Fleet
           ├── Driver
           └── Trip

Tetapi setiap domain tetap punya boundary sendiri.

BUSINESS LOGIC
        ≠
INFRASTRUCTURE

> "Apakah vehicle tersedia?"

> "Bagaimana membaca D1?"
