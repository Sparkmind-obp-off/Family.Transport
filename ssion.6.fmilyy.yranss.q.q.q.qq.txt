20. DOUBLE BOOKING PROTECTION
============================================================

This is CRITICAL.

The system must protect against two customers attempting to
reserve the same vehicle/resource for overlapping periods.

Availability check alone is NOT enough.

At reservation/confirmation time:

1. Recheck availability.
2. Perform operation within the appropriate transaction/
   concurrency boundary.
3. Prevent conflicting reservation.
4. Return a conflict response if unavailable.

Expected behavior:

Customer A → SUCCESS
Customer B → CONFLICT

Never allow both to silently succeed.
