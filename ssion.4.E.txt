4-D.3 — PUBLIC API

Endpoint yang bisa diakses tanpa login penuh:

GET /vehicles
GET /vehicles/:id
GET /services
POST /availability/check
POST /quotes/request

> public ≠ unrestricted.



Semua tetap harus melalui:

validation

rate limiting

abuse protection

GET /me
GET /me/bookings

GET /me/profile

> resource miliknya sendiri.

4-D.8 — AUTHENTICATION

Authentication menjawab:

> Who are you?



Pilihan:

MVP

Managed/session-based authentication.

atau secure server-side session.

Jangan menyimpan:

> password plaintext.




---

4-D.9 — AUTHORIZATION

Authorization menjawab:

> What are you allowed to do?



RBAC:

OWNER
ADMIN
OPERATOR
DRIVER
CUSTOMER

Contoh:

Customer:

GET /bookings/FT-123

Backend mengecek:

> booking.customer_id == current_user.customer_id



Kalau bukan:

403


---

4-D.10 — RBAC MATRIX

Action	Owner	Admin	Operator	Driver	Customer

View vehicles	✅	✅	✅	👁️	✅
Manage vehicles	✅	✅	❌	❌	❌
View bookings	✅	✅	✅	assigned	own
Manage pricing	✅	✅	limited	❌	❌
Manage users	✅	limited	❌	❌	❌
View payments	✅	✅	limited	❌	own
Assign driver	✅	✅	✅	❌	❌
View audit	✅	limited	❌	❌	❌
System settings	✅	limited	❌	❌	❌


limited harus diterjemahkan menjadi permission yang eksplisit saat implementasi.

4-D.16 — IDEMPOTENCY

Mutation penting:

POST /bookings
POST /payments
POST /refunds

menggunakan:

Idempotency-Key

Jika request dikirim dua kali:

> hanya satu transaction yang tercipta.




---

4-D.17 — RATE LIMITING

Public endpoint paling rentan:

POST /availability/check
POST /quotes/request
POST /bookings

Rate limit untuk:

> bot abuse



> spam



> brute force



> resource exhaustion




---

4-D.18 — BOT PROTECTION

Jika traffic meningkat:

WAF
+
Rate Limit
+
Bot Detection

MVP tidak perlu over-engineer.

Tetapi architecture harus siap dipasang di edge.


---

4-D.19 — CSRF

Jika menggunakan cookie-based authentication:

> CSRF protection wajib dipertimbangkan untuk state-changing requests.



Gunakan:

SameSite cookies


CSRF strategy yang sesuai framework/auth provider.

notes;
