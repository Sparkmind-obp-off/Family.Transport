4-C.6 — AVAILABILITY ENGINE

Formula utama:

AVAILABLE =
ACTIVE
AND SERVICE_COMPATIBLE
AND CAPACITY_OK
AND NO_BOOKING_CONFLICT
AND NO_MAINTENANCE_CONFLICT

Untuk with-driver:

+
DRIVER_AVAILABLE


---

4-C.7 — BOOKING OVERLAP

Dua interval conflict jika:

existing.start < requested.end
AND
existing.end > requested.start

Contoh:

Existing:

> 10:00–14:00



Request:

> 13:00–16:00



Result:

❌ CONFLICT


---

Request:

> 14:00–16:00



Jika sistem memakai interval [start, end):

✅ AVAILABLE

Ini penting supaya booking yang selesai tepat pukul 14:00 tidak bentrok dengan booking berikutnya pukul 14:00.


---

4-C.8 — MAINTENANCE CONFLICT

Sama:

maintenance.start < request.end
AND
maintenance.end > request.start

Jika overlap:

> vehicle unavailable.




---

DRIVER

+

> 10:00–14:00.

4-C.25 — QUOTE EXPIRATION

> valid until 18 Aug, 18:00.

---

4-C.27 — PRICE CHANGE

Misalnya quote:

> Rp800k



Saat customer accept:

Current pricing:

> Rp850k



System:

Option A

Honor original quote jika masih valid.

Option B

Require new quote.

Untuk MVP kita pilih:

QUOTE SNAPSHOT WINS

Jika quote masih valid dan resource masih available:

> customer mendapat harga quote tersebut.



Ini membuat customer experience lebih trustworthy.
