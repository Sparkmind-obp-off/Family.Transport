SESSION 2-D — COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION & DEFENSIBILITY

Tujuan utama:

> Bukan mencari slogan yang terdengar berbeda, tetapi membangun alasan nyata kenapa Family Transport layak dipilih dan semakin sulit ditiru.




---

2-D.1 — MASALAH UTAMA

Di market rental/transport, hampir semua competitor bisa mengatakan:

harga kompetitif;

mobil lengkap;

driver profesional;

terpercaya;

pelayanan ramah;

booking mudah.


Masalahnya:

> Semua itu mudah ditiru.



Jadi kita tidak boleh membangun positioning hanya dari klaim.


---

Kita bandingkan Family Transport terhadap market berdasarkan:

Dimension	Generic Rental	Family Transport Target

Fleet	✅	✅
Price	✅	Competitive
Driver	✅	✅
WhatsApp	✅	✅
Reviews	✅	Build

Clear Quote	⚠️	Strong
Customer Data	⚠️	Strong
Availability System	⚠️	Strong
CRM	⚠️	Strong
Corporate System	⚠️	Strong


🔥 Jadi differentiation bukan:

> “Kami punya mobil.”



Tetapi:

> “Kami membuat proses memilih dan mendapatkan transportasi menjadi lebih mudah.”

---

2-D.5 — MOAT #1

SERVICE MOAT

Service moat dibangun dari:

Response


Consistency


Clarity


Execution

Bukan sekadar:

> “Ramah.”



Tetapi:

> customer mendapatkan pengalaman yang konsisten dari inquiry sampai trip selesai.




---

2-D.6 — MOAT #2

DIGITAL MOAT

Kita membangun:

Customer Data

↓

Booking History

↓

Vehicle Performance

↓

Pricing Data

↓

Customer Behavior

↓

Better Recommendation

Semakin lama sistem digunakan:

> semakin banyak data.



Dan data tersebut meningkatkan keputusan bisnis.


---

2-D.7 — MOAT #3

OPERATIONAL MOAT

Fleet bukan hanya daftar mobil.

Kita tahu:

vehicle availability;

maintenance;

booking;

driver assignment;

utilization;

revenue per vehicle.


Contoh:

> Avanza A terlalu sering idle.



System memberi insight:

> pricing / promotion / utilization perlu diperbaiki.



🔥 Ini mulai menjadi operational intelligence.


---

2-D.8 — MOAT #4

TRUST MOAT

REAL BUSINESS

+

+
CLEAR TERMS
+
REAL CONTACT
+
CONSISTENT SERVICE

Kemudian:

Customer Reviews

Photos

Booking history

menjadi trust assets.

2-D.9 — MOAT #5

CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP

2-D.10 — MOAT #6

REPEAT BUSINESS

> Google → Booking

Kita buat:

BOOKING
↓
TRIP
↓
REVIEW
↓
CUSTOMER PROFILE
↓
REMARKETING
↓
REPEAT

Customer acquisition cost turun ketika repeat naik.


---

2-D.11 — MOAT #7

CORPORATE RELATIONSHIP

Corporate customer memiliki switching cost lebih tinggi.

Kalau Family Transport sudah menangani:

airport pickup;

employee trip;

client transport;

event transport;


maka relationship menjadi:

> ongoing business account



bukan:

> one-time rental.
