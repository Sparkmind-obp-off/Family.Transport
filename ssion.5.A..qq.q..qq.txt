5-A — PRODUCT SCOPE

5-A.1 Product Definition

Family Transport kita posisikan sebagai:

> Digital Transport Booking & Operations System



Bukan sekadar website rental.

   ↓

---

5-A.2 MVP OBJECTIVE

MVP harus membuktikan 3 hal:

1. CUSTOMER CAN BOOK

Customer bisa:

> menemukan layanan → request → mendapatkan quote → booking.



2. BUSINESS CAN OPERATE

Admin/operator bisa:

> mengelola vehicle → booking → driver → trip.



3. SYSTEM CAN PROTECT TRANSACTION

System mampu:

> availability + pricing + booking tanpa double booking.
