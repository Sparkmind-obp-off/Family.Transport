3-B.30 — CONVERSION FRICTION MAP

Kita identifikasi friction:

Stage	Potential Friction	Solution

Landing	Tidak tahu harus klik apa	Intent choices
Fleet	Terlalu banyak mobil	Smart filters
Vehicle	Tidak yakin cocok	Specs + recommendation
Availability	Mobil unavailable	Alternatives

Booking	Form panjang	Minimal fields
Confirmation	Takut tidak masuk	Booking ID
