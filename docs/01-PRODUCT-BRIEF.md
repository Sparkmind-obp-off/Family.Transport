# Product Brief — Family Transport Manager

## Product
Family Transport Manager

## Product type
Small internal transport operations tool.

## Problem
Transport coordination currently relies heavily on WhatsApp, calls, memory, and manual coordination.

## Opportunity
Provide one simple operational view so the operator can quickly answer:
- What trips are coming up?
- Who is the customer?
- Where and when is the trip?
- Which vehicle is assigned?
- Which driver is assigned?
- Is the trip confirmed?
- What still needs attention?

## Primary user
Family Transport operator.

## Secondary users
None in MVP. Drivers and customers continue using WhatsApp.

## Core value
Less scattered information and less mental load during daily coordination.

## Success criteria
The operator can create and update a trip in under a few minutes and understand the day's work without searching multiple chats.

## Constraints
- Small budget / one-off project
- Minimal training
- Mobile-first
- Simple UI
- No unnecessary automation
