5-H.15 — DOUBLE BOOKING
Ini salah satu risiko terbesar.
Scenario:
09:00: Customer A checks vehicle X
09:01: Customer B checks vehicle X
09:02: A books
09:02: B books
Tanpa concurrency protection:
A = SUCCESS
B = SUCCESS
🚨 Salah.

5-H.53 — RELIABILITY PRINCIPLE
Kita kunci:
A failed side effect must not corrupt the primary transaction.
Contoh:
Booking
  ↓
SUCCESS

WhatsApp
  ↓
FAILED
Result:
Booking = CONFIRMED
Notification = FAILED
bukan:
Booking = CANCELLED
5-H.54 — SECURITY PRINCIPLE
Dan:
A successful-looking client request is not evidence of a successful business transaction.
Hanya server/database transaction yang menentukan.
