4.21 — AVAILABILITY ENGINE

Availability tidak boleh hanya:

> vehicle.status === available



Karena vehicle bisa:

> available sekarang



tetapi sudah dibooking untuk tanggal depan.

Formula:

VEHICLE ACTIVE
+
NO CONFLICTING BOOKING
+
NO MAINTENANCE
+
SERVICE COMPATIBLE
=
AVAILABLE


---

4.22 — AVAILABILITY CONFLICT

Booking A:

20–22 August

21–23 August

System harus mendeteksi overlap.

Booking B:

> CONFLICT
